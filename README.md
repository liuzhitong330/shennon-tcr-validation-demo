# ShennonBio TCR validation demo

A role-specific interactive research note built by Cathy Liu for Shennon Biotechnologies' Research Associate / Senior Research Associate position.

The demo connects three responsibilities from the official posting:

- qualification of transduced primary T-cell batches;
- flow-cytometry and ELISA-based functional assay design;
- donor-balanced, antigen- and HLA-controlled co-culture planning.

Its evidence backbone is ShennonBio's 2025 SITC abstract on MAGE-A1 96–104:HLA-A*03:01 TCR candidates. Published observations are visually separated from illustrative planning calculations. No proprietary data or company SOPs are used.
